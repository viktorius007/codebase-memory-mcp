## Commit 2b9a55b6 (earlier hashes 2c35f457, a1d917b6): fix(rust): glob walks of a facade prelude cover every reachable module
- Mechanism (measured first): `Type::f` under `use bevy::prelude::*` (examples, 336 of the 372 G008 sites) stopped with function_not_in_registry. One name lookup kept its visited modules in a 64-entry set (RmtVisit). bevy::prelude globs bevy_internal::prelude, which globs every sub-crate prelude, and those glob their own modules.
